# Sell Mineral Rights Michigan — Site-Specific Rules

This project has its own Michigan mineral-production voice and its own Aquatech-derived visual system. Never import a reusable mineral-rights voice pack or reuse another site's hero, FAQ, related rail, article ending, CTA, or decorative language.

## Voice lock

Write as a Michigan mineral production ledger. Use Antrim biogenic-gas decline, long production tails, statement months, paid decimals, old farm reservations, county legal descriptions, fractional heir ownership, and a documented owner file. Never use first-person singular business narration, guaranteed value, instant-cash language, highest-offer claims, or promises of future drilling.

## Visual lock

Preserve Aquatech's real geometric sans typography, navy and cyan palette, asymmetric image mastheads, pale blue bands, four-part applications rail, editorial carousels, stat split, illustrated company cadence, and wide index grid. Slug pages use one wide hero image, broad readable production-led content, a Michigan ledger FAQ, connected-record related links, and a cyan owner-file CTA. Never insert another site's generic rounded card stack.

## Release blockers

- The street address may appear only inside a Google Maps embed query.
- No footer thumbnails, blank images, tiny images, floating media glitches, emoji, or Unicode-arrow decoration.
- Every slug has exactly one image: its hero image.
- No vertical one-character text, squeezed desktop columns, invisible copy, weak contrast, clipped sections, or overlapping header elements.
- Desktop header includes real routes, phone, and a clear contact CTA. Dropdowns work by hover and focus without a pointer gap; the mobile menu and every carousel control work.
- Contact forms contain exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Every footer contains a Google Maps embed.
- The homepage hero contains an SEO-driven H1.
- Every page retains the Aquatech-derived fonts and theme.

Completion requires both machine gates, full desktop/mobile screenshot review, production deployment, and live alias verification.
